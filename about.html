I’m Adrian, an Interactive Design student at Valencia College, born and raised in Central Florida. Growing up on the internet sparked my curiosity for digital experiences and shaped my passion for UI/UX and Web Design. Outside of design, you'll usually find me producing music, skateboarding, or diving into internet rabbit holes in search of new ideas and inspiration.

I’ve always been drawn to creative and tech-related hobbies: From experimenting with After Effects and Photoshop, to teaching myself how to make music on FL Studio, exploring front-end web development, and playing video games. I became especially interested in UI/UX because it sat at the intersection of creativity and technology. I enjoy turning ideas into digital experiences that are meaningful to the people using them.
